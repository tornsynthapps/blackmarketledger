# Introduction to Blackmarket Ledger

Welcome to the **Blackmarket Ledger** (BML) documentation!

Blackmarket Ledger is a premium trading logistics and profit-tracking tool designed specifically for players of [Torn](https://www.torn.com/). It allows you to track your item inventory, calculate cost bases, monitor trading profits, and review loss metrics (such as muggings) to help you make informed trading decisions.

## What is Blackmarket Ledger?

At its core, Blackmarket Ledger provides a centralized, high-performance dashboard to log your Torn transactions. It calculates:

- **Total Realized Profit**: Net gains from selling items.
- **Current Inventory Value**: The total cost basis of your current stock.
- **Total Mug Loss**: Money lost to muggers during trading.
- **Net Total Profit**: Your realized profit minus your mug losses.
- **Abroad vs Trading Profit**: Differentiating between sourcing gains and market fluctuations.

## Key Concepts

### ✈️ Abroad & Self-Sell

A unique feature of BML is the ability to separate "sourcing" profit from "trading" profit. By using the **Self-Sell** workflow, you can sell items to yourself at market rates (e.g., Weav3r pricelist) when you return from abroad. This isolates the profit you made by traveling from the profit you make by actively playing the market.

### 🤖 Auto-Pilot

BML features **Auto-Pilot**, an automated logging system that syncs directly with the Torn API. It reconciles trades with Weav3r receipts, processes market transactions, and tracks muggings without manual data entry.
