# Stats & Charts Guide

Visualize your trading performance with BML's interactive charts. This guide explains how to interpret the data and use the charting features effectively.

## 📈 Accessing Charts

Simply click on any **Stat Card** on the main dashboard to open its detailed trend modal.
- **Total Realized Profit**
- **Current Inventory Value**
- **Total Mug Loss**
- **Net Total Profit**

### Daily View (30 Days)

## 🔍 Understanding the Data

- **Cumulative Totals**: Most charts show cumulative values (e.g., Total Profit up to that date), giving you a clear picture of your overall growth.
- **Averages & Extremes**: Every modal displays your **Average**, **Highest**, and **Lowest** values for the selected period.
- **Net Profit Accuracy**: The Net Profit chart subtracts all historical muggings from your realized profit, ensuring your bottom line is always accurate.

## 🚀 Pro Tips

- **Compare Trends**: Compare your Inventory Value trend with your Profit trend. A rising inventory usually precedes a spike in profit!
- **Analyze Mugs**: Use the Weekly/Monthly Mug Loss charts to identify periods where you might need to adjust your security or trading patterns.
