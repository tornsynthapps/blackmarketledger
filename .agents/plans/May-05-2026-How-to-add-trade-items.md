
Calculations:

Firstly, when trade is added, fetch market price of all items, then calculate the total cost of received items based on the total market price.
Then, check percentage paid by us. Then calculate money paid per item.

For instance
Items Received: Teddy Bear Plushie: 10, Kitten Plushie: 20, Monkey Plushie: 30
Market Price: 10, 10, 30
Money Paid: 1080

Total Cost based on market price: 10 * 10 + 20 * 10 + 30 * 30 = 1200
Percentage paid by us: 1080 / 1200 = 0.9 (90%)

So, we paid 75% of the total cost of the items received.

Now, we calculate money paid per item.
For instance, if we received 10 Teddy Bear Plushies, MP = 10, MP * 0.9 = 9
Now for Kitten Plushies, MP = 10, Mp * 0.9 = 9
And for Monkey Plushies, MP = 30, MP * 0.9 = 27

Verifying the above calculations, we get the following results:
total paid = 10 * 9 + 20 * 9 + 30 * 27 = 1080
